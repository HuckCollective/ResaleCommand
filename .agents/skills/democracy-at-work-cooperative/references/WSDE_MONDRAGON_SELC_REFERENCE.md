# Co-op & WSDE Economic Governance Reference (`WSDE_MONDRAGON_SELC_REFERENCE.md`)

This reference codifies the foundational economic, federation, multi-channel, labor, and tax-allocation pillars governing **The Huckleberry Collective** and **Resale Command (`resalecmd.com`)** across `/coop/settlements`, `/coop/governance`, `/coop/labor`, `/expenses`, and `/coop`.

> **UI Wording Rule:** Always use institutional WSDE, Mondragon, and Rochdale/SELC terminology (`Worker Self-Directed Enterprise`, `Producers & Enablers of Surplus`, `Mondragon 10/45/45 Waterfall`, `Indivisible Commons Reserve`) in UI views rather than citing individual economists by personal name.

## 1. WSDE Surplus Theory & The 3 Cooperative Member Archetypes

- **Producers of Surplus:** Direct labor generating revenue through sourcing, restoration, pricing, booth/pop-up merchandising, and live sales.
- **Enablers of Surplus:** Infrastructure, software/AI automation, social media & Dropcast content creation, photography, online order fulfillment/shipping, bookkeeping, thermal barcode tagging, and retail space management that make surplus production possible.
- **Surplus Appropriation & Subsumed Class Distribution:**
  - Both **Producers** and **Enablers** co-govern the collective surplus on an equal `1 Member = 1 Vote` basis.
  - Enabler costs and fixed overhead (**Net Booth Rent, Pop-Up Table Fees, Tech & AI Ops, Shared Supplies**) are funded via a **Subsumed Class Distribution** (**30% Shared Commons Pool / Collective Reserve**) prior to bonus patronage payouts.

## 3. Mondragon Internal Capital Accounts (ICAs) & Federation Stabilization Rules

### A. Indivisible Collective Reserve & Shortfall Carryover
- **Indivisible Collective Reserve (Commons Pool):** An indivisible collective fund (`30%` default Gate 3 allocation) that covers fixed monthly operating costs (`$297.09` Net Booth Rent after `$85.41` Consignor offset + `$219.99` Tech Ops + `$6.18` Supplies = `$523.26/mo` baseline) and absorbs operating shortfalls.
- **Shortfall Carryover Rule:** Any monthly operating deficit (e.g., `-$132.97`) is carried forward as a Collective Reserve obligation (`carriedOverShortfall`) and repaid **100% first** in subsequent profitable months before Gate 4B bonus patronage dividends unlock.

### B. The Mondragon 10 / 45 / 45 Net Revenue Waterfall
- **Net Revenue Definition:** Gross sales minus direct material/production costs (Venue Commission + 100% Member Cost Basis Recoupment + Shared Operating Hurdle).
- **10% Inter-Co-op Coordination Fund:** Withdrawn first from excess surplus to fund shared central administration, network infrastructure, and mutual insurance/rainy-day reserve across partner co-op units.
- **45% Enterprise Collective Reserve / Sales Patronage:** Retained for inventory expansion, working capital, and pro-rata sales patronage.
- **45% Member Capital / Sweat-Equity Ledger:** Credited to individual worker-member accounts based on verified patronage and enabling labor hours. Retained internal capital balances earn a **7% annual cash yield/bonus** while providing working liquidity to the collective.

### C. Mondragon Governance & Pay Guardrails
- **Reversed Hierarchy:** The General Assembly (all worker-members, `1 Member = 1 Vote`) hires, evaluates, and can recall/terminate operational leads or managers. Financial prep, reconciliation, and governance meetings count as credited enabling labor time.
- **Open Procurement:** Co-op units solicit competitive bids for tools, thermal labels, packaging, and software inputs (best quality at lowest price) rather than subsidizing inefficient vendors.
- **Pay & Bounty Ratio Cap (`4.5:1` Standard / `6.5:1` Max):** Any hourly labor bounties or stipends between highest- and lowest-compensated roles are compressed to a **4.5:1** standard (and never exceed a **6.5:1** maximum exception).
- **Complementary Niche Non-Competition:** Partner co-ops within the collective network operate in complementary product niches rather than competing head-to-head in the same booth (`HUCK-` focuses on curated vintage apparel, oddities, decor & media; `PDXGL-` focuses on tabletop board games, RPGs & gaming accessories).

## 4. Personal IRS Schedule C Write-Off vs. Shared Co-op Commons Expense Separation

To prevent partners from subsidizing personal curator deductions in Gate 4A—and to prevent double-counting reimbursed expenses on IRS Schedule C—every expense in `/expenses` (`src/lib/coopExpenses.ts`) carries an explicit `scope`:

1. **`coop_shared` (`🤝 Co-op Shared — 100% Reimbursed from 30% Commons Pool`):**
   - Booth rent, shared Rollo thermal labels, booth display racks, shared Resale Command cloud/AI ops, pop-up event table fees.
   - Included `100%` in Gate 4A Co-op operating overhead (`CoopSettlementView.vue`).
2. **`personal_write_off` (`👤 Personal Write-Off — 100% Personal Schedule C, $0 Charged to Co-op`):**
   - Thrift/estate sale mileage & gas (`Hauling & Sourcing Transport`), Home Office / `HG` Garage / `HH` Hideout storage deduction (`IRC § 280A`), personal camera/phone gear, curator-only supplies.
   - **`$0.00` charged to the Co-op Commons Pool**; tracked `100%` in the member's Personal Schedule C Write-Off Ledger & CSV export.
3. **`split` (`⚖️ Split Co-op / Personal — e.g. 50% Co-op / 50% Personal`):**
   - Mixed-use subscriptions or trips; charges `coopSharePct%` to Gate 4A Co-op Commons and `(100 - coopSharePct)%` to the Personal Schedule C Write-Off Ledger.
