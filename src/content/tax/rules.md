### 1. Sourcing Mileage & Venue Travel (`Schedule C · Line 24a`)
Sourcing trips to thrift stores, estate sales, flea markets, auction pickups, and restocking runs to partner booths qualify for the standard IRS business mileage deduction. Logging **1,500 business miles** typically unlocks **$1,000+ in direct taxable income reduction**. Keep date, origin/destination, miles, and business purpose logged contemporaneously.

### 2. Booth Rent & Mall Commissions (`Schedule C · Line 20b & Line 10`)
Monthly vintage mall space rent (**Line 20b — Rent on Business Property**) and register consignment commissions (**Line 10 — Commissions & Fees**) are **100% deductible operating expenses**. For Collective curators whose rent is paid out of the 30% Commons Pool, those fees are already netted before payout so you are never taxed on gross register dollars you didn't keep.

### 3. Online Marketplace & Payment Fees (`Schedule C · Line 10`)
Final Value fees on eBay, Etsy, Poshmark, Mercari, payment processing cuts, listing upgrades, and promoted listing ad spend are **100% deductible** against gross platform receipts.

### 4. Shipping Postage & Packaging Supplies (`Schedule C · Line 22 & Line 27a`)
Commercial outbound shipping labels purchased via Pirate Ship, USPS, UPS, or marketplace label flows deduct under **Line 27a (Postage/Shipping)**. Boxes, poly mailers, bubble wrap, packing tape, hang tags, and Rollo thermal barcode labels deduct in full as **Supplies (Line 22)** in the year purchased.

### 5. Form 1099-K Gross vs. Net Trap (`Schedule C · Lines 1–2 & Cost of Goods Sold`)
> **Critical Audit Guardrail:** Online platforms report **gross buyer payments** (including shipping and refunded orders) on Form 1099-K. Always reconcile refunds/allowances (**Line 2**), platform fees (**Line 10**), outbound postage (**Line 27a**), and **Cost of Goods Sold (Part III)** so you never pay self-employment + income tax on pass-through money.

### 6. Software, POS & AI Scouting Credits (`Schedule C · Line 27a`)
Cloud hosting, accounting tools, barcode utilities, and Gemini AI scouting / OCR usage are **100% deductible** under **Other Business Expenses (Line 27a)**.

### 7. Cash Estate & Flea Purchases Without Paper Receipts (`Cohan Rule`)
Under *Cohan v. Commissioner*, cash purchases at garage sales, flea markets, or estate cleanouts remain deductible when supported by a **contemporaneous 5-point log**:
1. **Date** of purchase
2. **Item / Lot Description**
3. **Cash Amount Paid**
4. **Location / Seller** (estate address, swap meet, booth)
5. **Business Purpose + Haul Photo** attached in Purchases

### 8. Booth Fixtures & Hardware Safe Harbor (`De Minimis Election < $2,500`)
Under the **IRS De Minimis Safe Harbor Election (`Reg. §1.263(a)-1(f)`)**, tangible display cases, garment racks, lighting, mannequins, scales, and thermal printers costing **up to $2,500 per invoice/item** can be expensed **100% in Year 1** instead of depreciated over multiple years.
