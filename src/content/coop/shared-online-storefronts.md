## 1. Shared Co-op Online Accounts ("Digital Booth `#0EJ`")

Instead of forcing every member to maintain separate merchant accounts and label printers, the Collective operates a shared online storefront anchored to `thehc.llc`:

1. **eBay Business Account (`thehc.llc` EIN + LLC Bank) with Multi-User Account Access (`MUAA`):**
   - Stewards invite verified Curators under `Account Settings -> Permissions` with scoped operational permissions: **Create & edit drafts, Publish listings, View orders, and Print shipping labels**.
   - **Zero Banking Exposure:** Delegated MUAA members never see or modify LLC bank routing, tax settings, or payout schedules.
2. **Pirate Ship (`$0/mo` Subscription) + `Custom SKU` Attribution:**
   - Connected directly to the Co-op eBay store with **`Print Custom SKU on Label`** enabled.
   - Every listing sets its `Custom Label (SKU)` to the item's canonical barcode (`HUCK-1042`, `PDXGL-0218`), printing the exact SKU on the `4×6` Rollo thermal shipping label so postage costs reconcile `1:1` to the item in `/coop/settlements`.
3. **Transparent Online Cost Waterfall:**
   - **`<= 250` Listings/Month = `$0.00/mo` Fixed Store Cost:** Uses eBay's 250 free monthly zero-insertion-fee listings first. Only once active listings consistently exceed 250/mo does the Collective opt into a `$21.95/mo` Basic Store (reimbursed from the `30%` Commons Pool in Gate 4A).
   - **Per-Order Marketplace Fee (`~13.25%`) + Actual Pirate Ship Label Postage:** Deducted/recouped `100%` first in Gates 1–2 before computing Net Surplus.
   - **Shared Packing Supplies:** Boxes, poly mailers, bubble wrap, tape, and Rollo `4×6` thermal labels are logged as `🤝 Co-op Shared 100%` expenses in `/expenses` and reimbursed from the `30%` Commons Pool.

## 2. Dual-Option Backstock & Online Co-Curation (`HG` / `HH`)

Because every voting member also carries the foundational **`💎 Curator`** hat, any member can team up with an Original (`OG`) Buyer to unlock unplaced backstock in `HG` or `HH`. Before listing, both members agree on **one of two clean modes** per lot or item:

- **Mode A — `35% OG Buyer / 35% Active Lister` Split:**
  1. Venue/marketplace fee + outbound Pirate Ship label postage are paid first (Gate 1).
  2. OG Buyer recoups **`100%` of landed item cost basis** first (Gate 2).
  3. Of Net Surplus, the `70%` Curator share splits automatically **`35%` to the OG Buyer** and **`35%` to the Active Lister** (who cleaned, photographed, listed, and packed), with `30%` funding the Shared Commons Pool.
- **Mode B — Agreed Fair Transfer Price:**
  1. OG Buyer and Active Lister agree upfront on a fixed transfer floor (e.g., `$15.00`).
  2. When the item sells, OG Buyer receives **`100%` of the agreed transfer floor** in Gate 2, and Active Lister earns the full **`70%` Curator share** on all Net Surplus above that floor (`30%` to Commons Pool).

## 3. Four Ironclad Safeguards (Zero Double-Dipping, Zero Double-Sales)

1. **🚫 No Double-Dipping (`Gate 3 Profit` vs. `/coop/labor`):**
   - Prep, photography, listing, and packing on **your own inventory or a co-curated (`35/35` / `Transfer Price`) item** are paid by your Gate 3 Curator profit share—do **not** log those hours in `/coop/labor`.
   - `/coop/labor` (`1h = 1h`) is reserved strictly for **Shared Collective Commons work** (whole-booth resets, shared software/display builds, multi-partner Dropcasts, and Commons/consignor fulfillment).
2. **↩️ Online Returns = Next-Month Adjustment:**
   - Locked settlement months are never reopened for a standard eBay buyer return. Any refunded margin or return label postage is recorded as a **Next-Month Adjustment** against the original split.
3. **⏱️ 14-Day Co-Curation Claim Clock:**
   - When an Active Lister claims unplaced backstock from `HG` or `HH`, it must be photographed and published within **14 days**, or the claim automatically expires so the OG Buyer's capital is never stuck in limbo.
4. **📦 Physical Separation (`📦 Online Ready Bin`):**
   - Any item actively listed on eBay or Poshmark must be placed inside the **`📦 Online Ready Bin` in `HH` or `HG`** (never left loose on the `#0EJ` or `DT` mall shelf where a booth shopper could buy it at the same time).
