## 1. One Shared Online Shop (No Separate Accounts Needed)

Instead of asking every member to open separate business store accounts, track tax paperwork alone, or run separate shipping subscriptions, the Collective operates one shared online storefront under `thehc.llc`:

1. **Shared Collective eBay Shop with Member-Safe Access:**
   - Verified members receive scoped listing and shipping access (**create & edit drafts, publish listings, view orders, and print shipping labels**).
   - **Zero Banking Exposure:** Member access never exposes Collective bank routing, tax IDs, or master payout settings.
2. **Pirate Ship (`$0/mo` Subscription) + Automatic Barcode Attribution:**
   - Connected directly to our shared eBay shop with **`Print Custom SKU on Label`** enabled.
   - Each listing uses the item's personal barcode SKU (`DOM-1042`, `STORM-0218`, etc.), so every `4×6` thermal shipping label prints the SKU right on the label and reconciles postage automatically in `/coop/settlements`.
3. **Simple Online Cost Breakdown:**
   - **`<= 250` Listings/Month = `$0.00/mo` Fixed Store Fee:** We stay on free monthly listings until active volume justifies a paid store tier (covered cleanly from the `30%` Commons Pool).
   - **Marketplace Fee + Actual Shipping Label:** Paid first from the sale proceeds before profit is split, so no member loses money out-of-pocket on postage.
   - **Shared Packing Supplies:** Boxes, poly mailers, bubble wrap, tape, and `4×6` thermal labels are logged as `🤝 Co-op Shared 100%` expenses in `/expenses` and paid from the `30%` Commons Pool.

## 2. Teaming Up on Backstock (Co-Curation)

Every voting member is a **💎 Curator** with their own unique barcode prefix. When one member has unlisted backstock waiting for time or photography—and another member wants to prep, photograph, or list it—both members agree on **one of two simple splits** before publishing:

- **Option A — 50/50 Split of the Curator Profit (35% Sourcing Member / 35% Listing Partner):**
  1. Marketplace fee and shipping label are paid first.
  2. The **Sourcing Member** recoups **`100%` of what they paid for the item** first.
  3. The remaining profit splits **`35%` to the Sourcing Member** and **`35%` to the Listing Partner** (who prepped, photographed, listed, and packed it), with **`30%` supporting the Shared Commons Pool**.
  *(Smaller assist? Members can also swap simple Booth Prep or Photo & Stage help by mutual agreement using the same clear record.)*
- **Option B — Agreed Buyout / Transfer Floor Price:**
  1. Both members agree upfront on a fixed floor payout for the Sourcing Member (for example, `$15.00`).
  2. When the item sells, the Sourcing Member receives **`100%` of that agreed floor price** first, and the Listing Partner earns the full **`70%` Curator share** on everything above it (`30%` Commons Pool).

## 3. Four Simple Ground Rules

1. **🚫 No Double-Dipping (`Profit Share` vs. `Hourly Co-op Tasks`):**
   - Prepping, photographing, listing, and packing **your own items or a co-curated split item** is rewarded through your Curator profit share—do **not** also log those hours in `/coop/labor`.
   - `/coop/labor` (`1h = 1h`) is strictly for **shared Collective work** that benefits everyone (whole-booth resets, shared displays/software, group promotions, and Commons fulfillment).
2. **↩️ Online Returns Adjust Next Month:**
   - Closed settlement months stay locked forever. If an online buyer returns an item later, any refund difference or return postage is applied cleanly on the **next month's settlement**.
3. **⏱️ 14-Day Listing Window on Teamed-Up Stock:**
   - When a Listing Partner claims backstock to photograph and list online, it should go live within **14 days** so another member's cash isn't stuck waiting in limbo.
4. **📦 Physical Separation (`Online Ready Bin`):**
   - Any item actively listed online moves immediately into the **`📦 Online Ready Bin`** in our backstock—never left out on a mall booth shelf where a walk-in shopper and an online buyer could purchase the same piece twice.
