## 1. Low-Volume Cash Protection: The Bootstrapping Protocol

Operating physical antique mall booths requires strict protection of working cash reserves before liquid dividends unlock:

- **🔒 The `$3,000/mo` Cash Distribution Freeze:** Zero liquid cash dividends are drawn from the 30% Shared Commons Pool until the collective crosses **$3,000/month in gross sales over a rolling 3-month average** (`$9,000+` cumulative gross across 3 consecutive months).
- **🛡️ Overhead & Reserve Priority:** Prior to crossing the `$3,000/mo` rolling trigger, 100% of net Commons Pool surplus after booth rent (`$382.50/mo` at Memory Den `#0EJ`) and hosting/supplies stays locked inside the **Collective Reserve** to insulate rent runway.
- **📒 Permanent Sweat Equity Ledger:** During bootstrapping, skill partners and floor operators bank **Patronage Points** in our permanent cloud ledger (`resale_db`), establishing cumulative weight once quarterly cash distributions activate.

## 2. The Democratic Pitch & Vote Loop (`1 Member = 1 Vote`)

Instead of top-down task assignments, members propose high-impact micro-projects tied directly to live store bottlenecks:

1. **Data Transparency Check:** Members inspect live velocity metrics on `works.thehc.llc` (e.g., apparel out-selling media, barcode sync bottlenecks, slow mobile photo cropping).
2. **Targeted Pitch Submission:** A member pitches a clear proposal specifying **what it builds**, **how it accelerates physical sales or saves floor hours**, and **its Point Tier (`10`, `25`, or `50+`)**.
3. **4-Signal Loomio Consent Vote:** Applying `1 Member = 1 Vote`, the collective votes (`Agree`, `Abstain`, `Disagree`, `Block`). Patronage Points bank officially to the ledger **only upon verified production deployment or live campaign launch**.
