## 1. Core Alignment with the 70/30 Charter Blueprint

To protect physical inventory sourcers without diluting their direct capital risk, non-retail skill partners join the infrastructure layer inside the **30% Shared Commons Pool**. Skill partners contribute `$0` upfront inventory capital in exchange for **1 Member = 1 Vote** democratic governance rights and a share of the collective labor dividend pool.

| Membership Hat | Skill Pairing | Core Operational Focus | Compensation Stream |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Curator Partner** | Inventory Sourcers | Procuring physical stock at estate sales, auctions, and thrifts; mapping item velocity. | **100% Capital Recouped + 70% Net Profit Share** |
| **Surplus Enabler** | Floor & System Operators | Processing curator hauls, photographing items, maintaining Memory Den `#0EJ` layout, Rollo tagging, and fulfillment. | **Labor Dividends via 30% Commons Pool** |
| **Promoter** | Developers, Designers & Marketers | Maintaining Astro/Vue/Appwrite/Vercel stack, visual brand identity, lookbooks, and automated social Dropcast loops. | **Labor Dividends via 30% Commons Pool** |

## 2. Two-Step Prioritization Draw Inside the 30% Commons Pool

The **30% Shared Commons Pool** settles collective obligations before distributing labor dividends:

1. **First Draw — Fixed Material Overhead:** Pays physical booth rent at Memory Den (`$382.50/mo` gross before consignor pro-rata offset), Dusty Tiger (`$75.00/mo`), thermal label supplies, and baseline cloud/domain infrastructure (`thehc.llc`, Vercel, Appwrite).
2. **Second Draw — The Labor Dividends Pool:** Once fixed overhead (and any carried-over shortfall) is 100% covered and our bootstrap trigger is active, remaining surplus inside the Labor Patronage slice is distributed democratically to **Promoters** and **Surplus Enablers** based on verified logged contributions.

## 3. Built-In Operational Incentive Loop

Every technical and creative milestone directly compounds physical retail throughput:

- **Faster Software = More Tagged Stock:** When a developer ships a faster batch-upload or 2-way Ricochet barcode flow in Works, Curators place dresses, books, and collectibles onto shelves faster.
- **Stronger Merchandising = Higher Sell-Through:** When a designer upgrades booth signage or styles themed lookbooks, conversion rates and average ticket values climb.
- **Shared Upside:** As monthly gross sales grow, the dollar volume flowing into the 30% Commons Pool expands—directly growing quarterly labor dividends without taxing Curator cost basis.
