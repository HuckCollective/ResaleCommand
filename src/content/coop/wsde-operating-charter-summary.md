## 1. The 4-Gate Monthly Settlement Waterfall

Every settled sale in `works.thehc.llc` flows through four transparent accounting gates:

1. **Gate 1 — Venue Commission:** Deducts exact retail venue commission (`15%` at Memory Den `#0EJ`, `10%` at Dusty Tiger, platform fees online).
2. **Gate 2 — 100% Curator Cost Basis Recouped:** Returns 100% of verified item purchase cost (`costBasis`) directly to the sourcing Curator before any split occurs.
3. **Gate 3 — 70 / 30 Item Net Margin Split:** Splits net item surplus **70% to the Curator Partner** and **30% to the Shared Commons Pool** (while straight consignors like `BB-` settle via their dedicated Consignor Agreement).
4. **Gate 4 — Commons Overhead & Democratic Surplus:**
   - **Gate 4A (Overhead & Shortfall First):** Pays monthly booth rent, cloud/ops expenses, and 100% of any carried-over prior shortfall.
   - **Gate 4B (3-Way Excess Surplus):** Once reserves and bootstrap thresholds are satisfied, allocates excess surplus across **Collective Indivisible Reserve (`10%`)**, **Curator Sales Patronage (`60%`)**, and **Enabler & Promoter Labor Patronage (`30%`)**.

## 2. How 4-Signal Loomio Consent Voting Works

Proposals pass when they reach our minimum **Agree** threshold (`2+` Agree signals by default) with **zero active Blocks (`0` Vetoes)**:

- **🟢 Agree:** *"I support this proposal and want to proceed."* Counts toward positive consensus.
- **🟡 Abstain:** *"I trust the group's decision."* Neutral signal; does not block consensus.
- **🟠 Disagree:** *"I have reservations or suggestions, but will not block."* Requires a constructive comment so the proposer can refine if needed.
- **🔴 Block (Principled Veto):** *"This violates our WSDE charter, physical tag safety laws, or endangers collective solvency."* Pauses enactment until amended with a counter-proposal.
