Bring curated pieces under a transparent Straight Consignment agreement (pro-rata booth rent share, flat commission, or fixed monthly shelf rent) with automatic slow-month protection so slow sales months never produce a negative payout check. Consignors have zero co-op labor or governance obligations and view a clean, private statement scoped strictly to their own barcode prefix.
