### One Member, One Vote (Loomio 4-Signal Consent Protocol)

Every voting worker-member—whether contributing curated inventory (`Curator`), audience growth (`Promoter`), booth/shipping operations (`Surplus Enabler`), or stewardship (`Founder`)—holds **1 equal vote** in the General Assembly regardless of capital volume.

Proposals on waterfall splits, new retail booth leases, shared capital purchases, and consignor rate templates are ratified using four clear consent signals:

| Signal | Meaning | Governance Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🟢 Agree** | I support this proposal and help carry it forward. | Counts toward active quorum and passage. |
| **🟡 Abstain** | I stand aside and trust the group's decision. | Recorded for quorum without blocking consensus. |
| **🟠 Disagree** | I see a concern and suggest an amendment, without vetoing. | Prompts discussion and refinement before close. |
| **🔴 Block (Principled Veto)** | This change puts the Collective's safety, charter, or solvency at risk. | Halts passage immediately until revised with the blocking member. |

> **Open Strategy Library:** Foundational cooperative charters, dividend policies, and scaling playbooks live as versioned documents in `/coop/governance?tab=docs`, where any voting member can turn a document into a live proposal in one click.
