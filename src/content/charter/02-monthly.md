### Monthly Settlement Cadence & Automatic Rent Coverage

Partner retail venues pay the Collective **once a month** by check or direct deposit. At **{{PRIMARY_BOOTH_NAME}}**, the venue deducts its **{{PRIMARY_BOOTH_FEE}}% register fee** and **${{PRIMARY_BOOTH_RENT}}/mo booth rent** directly from gross register sales before issuing the net payout; at **{{SECONDARY_BOOTH_NAME}}**, the venue deducts **{{SECONDARY_BOOTH_FEE}}%** and **${{SECONDARY_BOOTH_RENT}}/mo**.

Under our **{{CURATOR_PCT}} / {{COMMONS_PCT}} waterfall**, the **{{COMMONS_PCT}}% Shared Commons Pool** absorbs monthly booth rent so individual curators never get hit with surprise out-of-pocket rent bills.

#### Across Online & Pop-Up Channels
- **Online Marketplaces (eBay, Etsy, Storefront):** Platform fees (12%–15%) and outbound postage labels are recouped at Gates 1–2; Curators keep **{{CURATOR_PCT}}%** of Net Surplus while **{{COMMONS_PCT}}%** funds poly mailers, boxes, thermal labels, and hosting.
- **Pop-Ups & Conventions:** Card processing (~2.9%) is deducted at Gate 1; the **{{COMMONS_PCT}}%** Commons Pool covers table fees, signage, and booth hardware.
