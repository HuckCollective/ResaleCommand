### How Sourcing & Shared Purchases Work

| Sourcing Path | Who Fronts Capital | How Proceeds Split |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Member Personal Curation (Most Common)** | You purchase inventory at thrift, estate sales, or auctions with personal funds. | **100% of your cost basis** (plus any online shipping postage) returns to you first at Gate 2, then you keep **{{CURATOR_PCT}}%** of the Net Surplus. |
| **Co-op Shared Purchases (Bulk Auction Lots)** | Purchased with the shared Huckleberry Collective operating account. | The Collective recoups **100% of lot cost** first, and Net Surplus rewards the members who unboxed, tested, photographed, and staged the haul. |
| **Straight Consignor Agreements** | External partners drop off inventory without co-op floor or voting duties. | Settled transparently under the partner's chosen agreement mode (pro-rata rent, flat commission, or fixed shelf rent) with a slow-month floor so checks never go negative. |

> **Why Cost-Back-First Matters:** Traditional antique mall subleases charge fixed rent even if nothing sells. In The Huckleberry Collective, sold inventory always returns your out-of-pocket cost dollar-for-dollar before surplus is shared.
