### Open-Book Commons Expense Reimbursement (Tier 1 Overhead)

Out-of-pocket operational expenses that benefit the whole Collective—such as **booth rent (${{PRIMARY_BOOTH_RENT}}/mo at {{PRIMARY_BOOTH_NAME}}, ${{SECONDARY_BOOTH_RENT}}/mo at {{SECONDARY_BOOTH_NAME}})**, Rollo thermal barcode labels, display showcases, garment racks, and shared AI scouting credits—are reimbursed **dollar-for-dollar from the {{COMMONS_PCT}}% Commons Pool** before surplus dividends distribute.

1. **1. Member Incurs Shared Cost:** A member fronting cash or card spend for shared booth supplies or fixtures uploads the receipt photo or PDF invoice in the Expenses workspace.
2. **2. Open-Book Verification:** Every logged receipt appears in the shared ledger with line-item attachment visibility so all voting members can audit collective overhead.
3. **3. Reimbursed at Settlement:** Monthly settlement repays verified Commons expenses first out of the {{COMMONS_PCT}}% Commons Pool before remaining surplus flows to Reserve and Labor Dividends.

### Commons Expenses vs. Personal Schedule C Deductions

| Expense Type | Paid / Reimbursed By | Tax Treatment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shared Booth Rent, Thermal Labels, Shared Fixtures & Cloud AI** | Reimbursed 100% from the **{{COMMONS_PCT}}% Commons Pool** | Deducted on the Collective's books so members are never taxed on reimbursed cash. |
| **Your Personal Sourcing Mileage, Home Staging Space & Personal Tools** | Kept **100% personal** by each independent partner | Deducted directly on your own **IRS Schedule C** against your 1099-NEC / resale income. |
