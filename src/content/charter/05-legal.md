### Cooperative Consortium Structure (Zero K-1 Tax Headaches)

- **Lean Legal Backbone:** The Huckleberry Collective LLC holds the retail booth leases and settlement clearing account while partners join under this Operating Charter as **Cooperative Associates**.
- **Simple Annual Tax Reporting:** Partner distributions over $600/yr are reported cleanly via **Form 1099-NEC**—eliminating $1,500+ partnership **Schedule K-1** CPA bottlenecks while preserving each curator's independent brand identity and barcode prefix.
- **Immutable Attribution:** Every physical tag carries an explicit partner barcode prefix so register CSVs attribute every sale automatically with zero manual guesswork.

### Five Combinable Collective Hats & Verified Sweat-Equity Labor

Members can wear one hat or combine several as their skills and schedule evolve:

1. **💎 Curator Partner:** Sources, prices, and places inventory; receives **100% cost basis back + {{CURATOR_PCT}}% Net Surplus** + full `1 Member = 1 Vote`.
2. **📣 Promoter ($0 Inventory Required):** Films Dropcasts, photographs drops, edits social reels, and drives foot traffic; earns pro-rata **Sweat-Equity Labor Pool** dividends + full `1 Member = 1 Vote`.
3. **🔥 Surplus Enabler ($0 Inventory Required):** Powers booth restocking, barcode tagging, haul intake, POS reconciliation, and online shipping; earns pro-rata **Sweat-Equity Labor Pool** dividends + full `1 Member = 1 Vote`.
4. **👑 Founder / Steward:** Maintains leases, monthly settlement reconciliation, tax compliance, and onboarding alongside active Assembly oversight.
5. **🏷️ Straight Consignor:** Hands-off inventory partner on a transparent contractual split or fixed shelf rent (no floor duties, labor pool draws, or governance obligations).
