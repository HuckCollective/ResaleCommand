### Cooperative Consortium Structure (Zero K-1 Tax Headaches)

- **Lean Legal Backbone:** The Huckleberry Collective LLC holds the retail booth leases and settlement clearing account while partners join under this Operating Charter as **Cooperative Associates**.
- **Simple Annual Tax Reporting:** Partner distributions over $600/yr are reported cleanly via **Form 1099-NEC**—eliminating $1,500+ partnership **Schedule K-1** CPA bottlenecks while preserving each curator's independent brand identity and barcode prefix.
- **Immutable Attribution:** Every physical tag carries an explicit partner barcode prefix so register CSVs attribute every sale automatically with zero manual guesswork.

### Six Combinable Collective Hats & Verified Sweat-Equity Labor

Members can wear one hat or combine several as their skills and schedule evolve (and every voting invite automatically includes **💎 Curator** so anyone can sell or co-curate items anytime):

1. **💎 Curator Partner:** Sources, prices, and sells inventory across online drops, pop-ups, events, and booths; receives **100% cost basis back + {{CURATOR_PCT}}% Net Surplus** + full `1 Member = 1 Vote`.
2. **📣 Promoter ($0 Inventory Required):** Films Dropcasts, promotes live auctions & pop-up events, edits social reels, and drives traffic; earns pro-rata **Sweat-Equity Labor Pool** dividends + full `1 Member = 1 Vote`.
3. **🔥 Surplus Enabler ($0 Inventory Required):** Powers online packing/shipping, pop-up & event staffing, barcode tagging, haul intake, and booth restocking; earns pro-rata **Sweat-Equity Labor Pool** dividends + full `1 Member = 1 Vote`.
4. **🛠️ Builder ($0 Inventory Required):** Builds web/AI tooling, brand signage, custom event tents/racks, and retail display fixtures; earns equal `1h = 1h` **Sweat-Equity Labor Pool** dividends + full `1 Member = 1 Vote`.
5. **👑 Founder / Steward:** Maintains legal/tax compliance, monthly settlement locks, venue & market contracts, and onboarding alongside active Assembly oversight.
6. **🏷️ Straight Consignor:** Hands-off inventory partner on a transparent contractual split or fixed shelf rent (no floor duties, labor pool draws, or governance obligations).
