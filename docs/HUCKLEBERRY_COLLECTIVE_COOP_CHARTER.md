# The Huckleberry Collective
## Worker Cooperative & Partner Collaboration Charter
*A Democratic, Transparent, and Shared-Equity Economic Engine*

---

### **1. Executive Summary: What is the Huckleberry Collective?**

The **Huckleberry Collective** is an independent **Worker Self-Directed Enterprise (WSDE)** and cooperative retail consortium. 

Instead of individuals struggling alone with high commercial booth rents, fragmented inventory tracking, and opaque consignor relationships, the Collective creates a **shared commons**:
- **Shared Prime Retail Booths**: Multi-channel physical presence (such as Memory Den in Portland, antique malls, and curated pop-ups).
- **Shared Professional Infrastructure**: Commercial direct-thermal barcode printing, live online cataloging, automated Ricochet POS syncing, and multi-channel dropcasting.
- **100% Financial Transparency**: Every sale, receipt, fee, and dollar is tracked on an open, auditable digital ledger.
- **Democratic Governance**: Members and partners work as equals. We operate on a **"Democracy at Work"** model where surplus is shared equitably based on the actual goods and labor contributed.

---

### **2. Why Partner With Us? The Collaborative Advantage**

Running a solo retail antique booth or vintage pop-up is hard:
- Monthly booth rents ($300 – $800/month) eat your margins if you have a slow week.
- You have to do all the sourcing, thermal tagging, shelf staging, dusting, and inventory audits yourself.
- Most consignment shops charge steep 30%–50% fees and provide little visibility into what sold and when.

#### **The Collective Solution:**
1. **Dramatically Lower Overhead**: Shared rent and supplies mean you only pay your proportional share of the physical space you use.
2. **Automated Barcode & Sales Tracking**: Your items are tagged with your own dedicated barcode prefix (e.g. `PDXGL-` for **PDX Game Library**).
3. **Discrete, Protected Ledgers**: Your inventory and payouts are never scrambled with anyone else's. When an item rings up at the register, our software automatically matches the sale to your account.
4. **100% Cost Recovery First**: When your items sell, your initial out-of-pocket inventory cost is returned to you **dollar-for-dollar** before surplus is calculated.
5. **Surplus Dividends**: Operating profit is distributed democratically—not hoarded by an owner or middleman.

### **3. How It Works in Practice (Partner Walkthrough)**

```mermaid

    A[1. You Bring Goods<br/>Tabletop, Vintage, Art] --> B[2. Thermal Barcode Tagged<br/>Your Dedicated Prefix e.g. PDXGL-]
    B --> C[3. Staged on Floor<br/>Memory Den Booth]
    C --> D[4. Customer Buys at Register<br/>Ricochet POS]
```

#### **Step 1: Your Dedicated Prefix**
You are assigned a unique, collision-free organization barcode prefix (e.g. `PDXGL-` for PDX Game Library, `HUCK-` for Huck Collective, or your own custom tag).

#### **Step 2: Tagging & Pricing**
You retain complete pricing autonomy. You decide the retail price and minimum acceptable sale price. The collective generates scannable Code128 thermal labels with your prefix and the venue register SKU.

#### **Step 3: Staged on the Floor**
Your goods are showcased in prime, high-foot-traffic booth spaces curated by the collective.

#### **Step 4: Transparent POS Reconciliation**
When venue sales reports arrive (e.g. Ricochet POS payout CSVs), our reconciliation engine parses every transaction:
- Matches exact barcode and item title.
- Verifies the store commission (e.g. Memory Den 15%).
- Separates your partner earnings onto your dedicated ledger.

#### **Step 5: Direct Payout**
You receive your complete itemized sales report with check numbers, sold dates, and direct net payout.

### **4. The Cooperative Financial Model**

We use a simple, battle-tested 3-tier cooperative waterfall:

1. **Tier 1 — The Shared Commons Pool (Covered First)**:
   Top-line revenue first pays the mandatory venue fees (e.g. 15% POS split) and proportional booth rent/supplies. No individual member is left on the hook for rent.
2. **Tier 2 — Capital Basis Recovery**:
   100% of your recorded out-of-pocket acquisition cost is returned directly to you.
3. **Tier 3 — Equitable Surplus Sharing**:
   Remaining profit is shared cooperatively based on:
   - Contributed inventory sales volume.
   - Documented labor hours (e.g. booth setup, intake, merchandising).
   - Reinvestment into collective equipment (better printers, signage, display cases).

### **5. Cooperative Governance: Democracy at Work**

We believe that workplace democracy builds stronger, more durable businesses.

- **1 Member, 1 Voice**: Every active collective member has an equal voice in decisions, regardless of how much capital they contributed.
- **Consent Decision-Making (Loomio Protocol)**:
  - 🟢 **Agree**: Support the proposal.
  - 🟡 **Abstain**: Neutral / trust the group.
  - 🟠 **Disagree**: Raise a concern to explore alternatives.
  - 🔴 **Block**: Veto only if a proposal violates our collective values.
- **Decisions We Vote On**: Expanding to new booth venues, purchasing major shared assets, adjusting seasonal commission splits, and welcoming new partner curators.
